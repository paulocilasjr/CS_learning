# Story Bible

The learner is a new Rebel technician whose capability grows into independent engineering responsibility. Leia frames objectives and consequences, R2-D2 models inspection and debugging, Han emphasizes practical readiness, and Chewbacca grounds maintenance tasks.

## Campaign arcs

1. **Leia's First Orders** — enter the Rebel computer and learn location, inspection, and navigation.
2. **Secrets of the Falcon** — read hidden information and create organized work areas.
3. **The Rescue Setup** — move, copy, locate, and verify mission data.
4. **The Secret Transmission** — reduce a flood of Imperial data to useful evidence.
5. **The Intelligence Machine** — connect tools because manual processing no longer scales.
6. **Build Your First Droid Program** — represent ship state as named data and expressions.
7. **Teach the Droid to Think** — encode safe decision rules.
8. **The Galactic Scanner** — automate repeated scans over collections.
9. **The Rebel Engineering Corps** — name reusable behavior and solve algorithmic problems.
10. **The Broken Navigation Computer** — investigate failures and verify repairs.
11. **The Rebel Code Repository** — preserve and combine work across engineers.
12. **The Final Campaign** — protect the fleet through independent, combined solutions.

## Tone rules

- Keep explanations concrete, brief, and respectful.
- Let technical actions cause story changes.
- Treat mistakes as evidence and retries as normal engineering.
- Avoid violence as a reward; emphasize protection, rescue, communication, and repair.
- Never imply that speed is more valuable than careful reasoning.

Story content may change without changing the skill graph. Technical engines must not depend on character names or plot flags.
